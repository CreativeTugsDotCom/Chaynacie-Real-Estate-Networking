Primary User Journeys & Connection Logic

End-to-end task flows designed for real-estate professionals seeking networking matches and seamless outreach.

Flow 1: New User Onboarding & First Network Outreach

1 Launch App → View 3-step value carousel → Tap "Get Started"
2 Sign up with Email/Password or Social auth provider
3 Select Primary Professional Role (e.g. Realtor, Lender, Investor)
4 Enter Location (e.g. Elyria, OH), Company & Short Bio
5 Land on Home Dashboard → Tap recommended professional profile
6 Tap "Connect" → Send optional introduction message → Connection Pending

Flow 2: Connection State Machine Logic

State A: Unconnected
Primary CTA: "Connect". Full profile preview visible according to privacy settings.
State B: Connection Pending
Primary CTA changes to "Request Pending". User can withdraw request. Recipient receives notification.
State C: Connected
Status changes to "Connected". Direct Messaging unlocked. Full contact preferences exposed.
State D: Monetization Trigger (If Freemium Cap Exceeded)
If free user reaches daily connection limit (e.g. 5/day), prompt Subscription Modal.

Monetization Architecture Options

The client selected 4 monetization avenues during initial inquiry. Here is how each option integrates into the mobile UX without causing friction.

1. Paid App Download

Upfront Pay

App costs $2.99–$9.99 on Apple App Store / Google Play before installation.

UX Strategy Tradeoff:
High friction for growth. Professional networking apps rely on network effects; paid downloads significantly reduce initial user acquisition in Ohio markets.

2. In-App Advertising

Ad Supported

Native sponsored profiles or banner placements (e.g. Title Companies, Mortgage Brokers, Inspection services).

Recommended UX Placement:
In-feed non-intrusive native cards on Home and Discover screens, labeled clearly as "Sponsored Partner".

3. Subscription (Freemium)

Recommended

Free tier gives unlimited search and basic connections. "Pro Networker" tier ($14.99/mo) unlocks unlimited connection requests, verified badge, and priority placement.

High-Value UX Model:
Allows organic community building in Elyria & Ohio while monetizing active high-volume realtors & lenders.

4. In-App Purchases (Microtransactions)

Pay-per-Action

Buy "Profile Boosts" for 24 hours of top search visibility, or purchase direct connection credits.

Flexible Monetization:
Great option for casual users who do not want a monthly recurring commitment.

Client Requirements Discovery Flags

These critical questions must be confirmed with Chaynacie during planning before proceeding to production development.

Target Audience & Access Rules

  • Is networking strictly restricted to licensed professionals (e.g. state license required for Realtors/Lenders), or open to any user?
  • Should property buyers and home sellers be permitted to join and contact professionals?
  • Is the initial release target focused specifically on Northern Ohio / Elyria, or planned for immediate nationwide availability?

Social & Feature Scope

  • Should users connect bidirectionally (like LinkedIn) or follow unidirectionally (like X/Twitter)?
  • Is direct 1-on-1 messaging required for MVP, or should connections exchange external contact details (phone/email)?
  • Should professionals be able to post social updates, articles, or market announcements in a feed?
  • Are local real estate events, webinars, or group discussion boards part of the core vision?

Property & Listing Scope

  • Should professionals be allowed to feature property listings on their profiles or in search?
  • Or should the application strictly avoid property listings to remain a pure professional networking platform?

Monetization Priority

  • Which revenue stream should be prioritized for V1: Free app with Subscription, Sponsored Ads, or Paid Download?
  • What exact benefits should be included in the paid subscription tier?
